Privacy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This notice explains what information the Acclimate Archery website handles and what happens to it. In short: the site itself doesn't collect anything about you. There are no forms, accounts, cookies, analytics or ad trackers, and nothing is saved in your browser.

Who runs this site

Acclimate Archery, run by Jake Bell in Steamboat Springs, Colorado. Questions about this notice or your information: jakebell412@gmail.com.

What's collected and why

  • Visiting the site. Our hosting provider, Vercel, automatically handles basic technical information, like your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for, so it can deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We don't use this to identify you.
  • Booking a call. The "Book a Call" and "Get in Touch" buttons open Cal.com, a separate scheduling service. Whatever you enter there (for example your name, email address and notes) goes to Cal.com and to Jake, and is used to schedule your call and get in touch with you about it.
  • Emailing or calling. If you email or call, we receive what you send us, such as your email address, phone number and message, and use it to reply to you. Email is handled by Google (Gmail).

We keep messages and booking details only as long as we need them to help you and to keep ordinary business records.

Who it's shared with

Only the services that make the above work. Each handles information under its own privacy policy:

  • Vercel (website hosting): vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Cal.com (call booking): cal.com/privacy
  • Google (email): policies.google.com/privacy

We may also share information if the law requires it. The site links to some brands' websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this notice doesn't cover.

We don't sell your information

We don't sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Getting a copy or having it deleted

To ask for a copy of the information we have about you, or to ask us to delete it, email jakebell412@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, we'll update it here and change the "Last updated" date at the top.
