Jake Bell — Owner / Founder

Jake Bell has spent over 15 years bow hunting across the US, and somewhere along the way, that passion turned into a career. When he's not in the field, he's usually still thinking about it — tuning bows, testing new setups, or growing his business. He's also an avid fly fisherman and a self-taught entrepreneur who built Acclimate Archery from the ground up.

Jake is known for his precision work — bow tuning and custom builds that are dialed in exactly to how you shoot. He's the guy people trust to get their setup right, whether that means fine-tuning before season or building something from scratch. Ask anyone who's worked with him and they'll tell you the same thing: he's sharp, easy to talk to, and genuinely wants to see you succeed out there.
